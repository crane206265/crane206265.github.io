About

I am an undergraduate student in Physics at KAIST, double majoring in Computer Science and Mathematical Sciences. My research focuses on radio astronomy and computational astrophysics, particularly VLBI, pulsar timing arrays, compact objects, and astronomy-related inverse problems.

I am studying physics to understand the universe, computing to solve those problems, and mathematics to understand their underlying structure. Since summer 2025, I have participated in undergraduate research at ObsCAL (Observational Cosmology and Astronomy Lab) at KAIST, first through an individual study and, from June 2026, through the Undergraduate Research Program.

ObsCAL, KAIST · May 2026-Present

VLBI-Assisted Localization of Continuous Gravitational-Wave Sources

Investigating how VLBI astrometric information can improve the localization of continuous gravitational-wave sources with SKAO-era pulsar timing arrays, with the eventual goal of identifying possible host galaxies of supermassive black-hole binaries.

ObsCAL, KAIST · Jul. 2024-Aug. 2026

Neural-Surrogate-Guided Asteroid Lightcurve Inversion

Developing a neural-surrogate-guided optimization framework that sequentially modifies a three-dimensional asteroid shape to reproduce multiple observed lightcurves.
