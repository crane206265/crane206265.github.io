About

I am an undergraduate student in Physics at KAIST, double majoring in Computer Science and Mathematical Sciences. My interests connect compact objects and gravitational waves with numerical simulation, optimization, and scientific machine learning.

I have worked at KAIST's Observational Cosmology and Astronomy Lab (ObsCAL) since June 2025, first through an individual study and, from June 2026, through the Undergraduate Research Program. During a summer 2026 internship at KASI, I developed atmospheric opacity and zenith total delay forecasts for autonomous KVN scheduling.
