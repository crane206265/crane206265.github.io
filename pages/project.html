Observational Cosmology and Astronomy Lab, KAIST · Jun. 2026-Present · Undergraduate Research Program

VLBI-Assisted Localization of Continuous Gravitational-Wave Sources

Developed Fisher-matrix forecasts of continuous gravitational-wave localization with VLBI-informed pulsar-distance priors, including thermal and ionospheric astrometric errors. Compared 300- and 1600-MHz strategies, identified high-impact pulsars, and assessed host-galaxy candidates using galaxy catalogs.

The project studies how pulsar-distance and astrometric priors affect pulsar-term phase uncertainty and localization performance in the SKAO era. It also frames a resource-allocation question: which pulsars should be prioritized for VLBI observations if the goal is to reduce host-galaxy candidate regions.

Undergraduate Individual Study, ObsCAL, KAIST · Jun. 2025-Jun. 2026

Neural-Surrogate-Guided Sequential Reshaping for Asteroid Lightcurve Inversion

Developed neural-surrogate-guided sequential shape reconstruction from synthetic asteroid lightcurves using learned reward maps and local shape deformations. Analyzed photometric degeneracy through PCA of reconstructed shape families, identifying modes with weak lightcurve sensitivity.

The work focuses on improving initial conditions and sequential action selection while reducing the need for expensive forward-model evaluations. The project also investigates the non-uniqueness of shape reconstruction under limited observation geometries.

Korea Astronomy & Space Science Institute · Jul.-Aug. 2026 · Undergraduate Research Internship

Atmospheric Forecasts for Autonomous KVN Scheduling

Developed GEFS-based atmospheric opacity and zenith total delay forecasts for autonomous Korean VLBI Network (KVN) scheduling, using machine-learning corrections.

Evaluated baseline differential delay, calibrated ensemble uncertainty, and seasonal high-opacity detection risks for VLBI observations. Presented as PAT07 at the 2026 Korean Astronomical Society Fall Meeting.

Research Project, KAIST · Mar. 2025-Present

Optimized Observation and Routing for Telescopes

Developing optimization-oriented methods for telescope observation planning and routing, with the goal of reducing operational overhead such as unnecessary slewing time while accounting for observational constraints.

My contributions focus on algorithmic planning and analysis related to observation constraints such as moonlight and expected signal-to-noise ratio.
